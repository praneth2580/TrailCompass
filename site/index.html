Features

Made for long days in the saddle

  • Routes for bikes

    Choose Fastest, Shortest or Less traffic and compare up to three routes. Expressways closed to two-wheelers are avoided unless you allow them.

  • Voice turn-by-turn

    Spoken prompts timed to your speed, straight into your helmet headset. Miss a turn and the route recalculates on its own.

  • Keeps going on weak signal

    Routes are calculated on your phone. Once a route is planned, turn-by-turn, voice prompts and stop suggestions work without a connection.

  • Built for the handlebar

    A full-screen ride view while you're moving, a screen that stays on, a compass, and large touch targets that are easy to hit on the move.

  • Save and share rides

    Keep trips in your library and open them later without planning again. Share a route as a file with your riding group.

  • Fresh data, no reinstall

    Fuel and food places are refreshed every month and download on their own. No need to update the app for new data.

Fuel & food

Stops that fit your ride

Tell TrailCompass your tank range and it picks pumps near your route in the last stretch of each tank, then plans the next leg from there. If a stretch has no pump, it tells you to fill up earlier.

Set when you leave and it finds breakfast, lunch or dinner where you'll be at that hour. Every place is built into the app, so suggestions work offline.

How it works

Plan, preview, ride

  1. Plan

    Search for your destination or drop a pin on the map. Add fuel, food or your own stops in the order you want them.

  2. Preview

    Compare up to three routes by distance and time. Save the one you like, or share it with the group.

  3. Ride

    Tap Start and follow the voice prompts. Go off route and it recalculates, keeping the stops you haven't reached yet.

Install

Install in a minute

  1. On your Android phone, tap Download. The file is about 10 MB.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, let your browser install apps (Install unknown apps).
  3. Tap Install, open TrailCompass and allow location access.

TrailCompass isn't on Google Play yet, so Android may warn you about installing an app from outside it.

FAQ

Questions riders ask

Is TrailCompass free?

Yes. There's no account to create and no ads.

Does it work offline?

Partly. Plan your route while you have a connection: the roads are saved on your phone, and turn-by-turn, voice prompts and fuel and food suggestions then work without signal. The background map needs a connection to load areas you haven't viewed.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 6.0 or newer. There is no iPhone version.

Why isn't it on Google Play?

It isn't listed there yet. Each release is published on GitHub, and the download button always gets the newest one.

How do I update?

Download the newest file and install it over the app you have. Fuel and food data updates on its own every month, without a new install.

Where does the map and place data come from?

Maps, roads and search come from OpenStreetMap, through OpenFreeMap, Overpass and Nominatim. Fuel stations and places to eat come from the Overture Maps Foundation and OpenStreetMap.

Does it collect my data?

There's no account and no analytics. To show the map and plan routes, the app downloads data for the areas you use from the open-data services above.
